Pelin Kıncır is a Master's student in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology at ETH Zurich, specializing in Signal Processing and Machine Learning. For her thesis, she is a visiting researcher (MIT affiliate) at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard with Prof. Giovanni Traverso, developing an AI-powered AR assistant for wet-lab procedures on Apple Vision Pro. Her research focuses on 3D perception and multimodal systems that assist people in physical environments. She holds a B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Boğaziçi University.
